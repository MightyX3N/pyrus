# Alletiders Nisse

A fan remake of **Alletiders Nisse**, the Pyrus Christmas calendar CD-ROM, as a Garry's Mod gamemode.
*En fan-genindspilning af Pyrus' julekalender – på dansk og engelsk.*

Open a door in the calendar every day in December, play the day's game, earn pebernødder and decorate
the tree with what you buy in Pyntekassen. The seasons are open all year.

## What's in it

- **The Christmas calendar** – all 24 door games, with the original voices, music and sound
- **Two looks** – a modern interface, or *classic*, like the CD-ROM
- **Pyntekassen and the Scrapbog** – decorations, pictures, trophies and highscores
- **Summer holiday** – Surfing, Is-tårnet, Fiskekonkurrencen and Sandslottet
- **Halloween** – Græskarskæring, Spøgelsesjagt, Slik eller ballade and Spøgelseslabyrinten
- **Easy, Normal and Hard**, with highscores and bronze, silver and gold trophies
- **Duel** – two players on one keyboard, split screen
- **Kapløb** – race your friends when you host it on a server

## How to play

Subscribe, then pick **Alletiders Nisse** in the gamemode list in the bottom right corner of the main
menu and start a game on any map.

Also playable in the browser at **https://pyrus.lol** – source on GitHub: https://github.com/MightyX3N/pyrus
